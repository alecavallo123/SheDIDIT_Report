Annex A: Interview Guide Sample

Section 1: Personal and Professional Background

  1. Could you tell us a bit about yourself: where you are from, how long you have been in Belgium, and what your life looks like today?
  2. What motivated you to start your own business, and when did you first have the idea?
  3. What have been the most significant challenges you faced in setting up or growing your business?

Section 2: Discovering #SheDIDIT and the Application Process

  1. How did you first hear about #SheDIDIT and the #SheFUND program?
  2. What made you decide to apply? What were your expectations at that point?
  3. Could you walk us through your experience of the application and preparation process?
  4. Did you receive coaching or mentoring as part of the process? What was most valuable?

Section 3: The Decision Moment

  1. How did you find out about the decision? What was your reaction?
  2. What did the pitching experience feel like? How would you describe the atmosphere and the jury?
  3. What did the decision mean to you, beyond the financial dimension?

Section 4: Post Decision Trajectory

  1. How has your business developed since the program?
  2. Has the experience changed how you see yourself as an entrepreneur or how you present yourself to others?
  3. Has your relationship with #SheDIDIT continued? How?

Section 5: Reflections and Recommendations

  1. If you could change one thing about the #SheFUND process, what would it be?
  2. What advice would you give to another migrant woman entrepreneur who is considering applying?
